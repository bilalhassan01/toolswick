## Why Client-Side Matters

A business card still opens doors: handed over at a meeting, it carries your name, your role, and how to reach you long after the conversation ends. Many card design sites ask you to create an account and store your contact details on their servers before you can download anything. This maker works differently. Your name, phone number, email, and company details are drawn onto a canvas inside your own browser and never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and you can design a card without handing your personal details to a stranger's server.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Designing your card takes a few minutes. First, type in your details: company name and tagline, your full name and job title, then phone, email, website, and address. Second, pick one of the four themes — Modern Minimal in dark, Executive Navy, Clean Studio in white, or Luxury Gold and Charcoal — and watch both the front and back previews update as you type. The front carries your company identity, while the back carries your personal details. Third, download the front and back as separate high-resolution PNG files, or click Print to produce a correctly scaled sheet for card stock.

## Feature Deep Dive

The card follows the standard 3.5 by 2 inch format, rendered at 700 by 400 pixels, so it matches what print shops expect and looks sharp in a digital portfolio too. The front and back are designed as a pair, with matching typography and colours, so the card feels like one coherent piece rather than two unrelated designs. Each theme is a complete look: Modern Minimal keeps things dark and understated, Executive Navy suits formal and corporate roles, Clean Studio is bright and simple, and Luxury Gold and Charcoal adds a premium feel for consultants and high-end services. Because the card is drawn on a canvas in your browser, every change appears instantly in the preview, and the downloaded PNG files are ready to send to a printer or share by email.

## Use Cases

Freelancers and consultants use the maker to produce a card before a first client meeting, without waiting days for a designer. Small business owners print cards for trade shows, markets, and networking events, where a well-made card is often the only thing a new contact takes home. Job seekers add a card to their application kit for career fairs and interviews. Students and recent graduates use one to introduce themselves at industry events. It is also handy for anyone who needs a replacement card in a hurry — a lost wallet or a changed phone number no longer means a week without cards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What should I put on the front of the card?**

The front carries your company name and tagline, so it works as a brand statement. Your name, title, and contact details go on the back, where the person you meet will look for them.

**Can I change the theme after entering my details?**

Yes. Switching themes never clears your text — the same details are redrawn instantly in the new style, so you can compare all four looks before choosing one.

**Are the downloaded files good enough for a print shop?**

Yes. The cards are rendered at 700 by 400 pixels in the standard 3.5 by 2 inch ratio, which suits most print shops. Ask your printer about bleed and card stock if you want a premium finish.

**Can I print the cards at home?**

Yes. The Print button produces a correctly scaled sheet, so you can print on card stock from a home or office printer. For the best result, use heavy paper and set your printer to its highest quality.
