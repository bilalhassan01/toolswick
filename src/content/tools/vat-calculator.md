## Why Client-Side Matters

VAT figures go straight onto invoices and into accounts, and working them out should not mean sending your prices to a server. This tool calculates VAT entirely inside your own browser, on your device. Nothing you enter is uploaded, stored, or shared, and nothing remains behind when you close the tab. You can price a quote or check an invoice in complete privacy.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Start by choosing "Add VAT" to add tax to a net price, or "Remove VAT" to extract the tax from a gross total. Then enter the amount. Next, pick a preset rate, such as the UK rate of 20 percent or a European rate of 19 percent, or type your own custom rate into the field. The tool immediately shows the net amount, the VAT value, and the gross total, all three at once. Change the amount or the rate at any time and every figure updates instantly.

## Feature Deep Dive

Adding VAT is straightforward: the tool multiplies the net price by the rate and adds the result to get the gross total. Removing VAT is the part people usually get wrong, because you cannot simply subtract the percentage from the gross price. Instead, the tool divides the gross price by one plus the rate, which recovers the original net amount exactly, and the difference between the two is the VAT. Preset rates cover the common ones, including the UK at 20 percent and a European rate at 19 percent, while the custom rate field accepts any percentage, so the tool works for any country or any special rate. Showing net, tax, and gross together means you can copy the right figure straight onto an invoice without doing a second calculation.

## Use Cases

Freelancers and small businesses use the calculator to add VAT to quotes and invoices, and to check that a supplier's invoice has the tax worked out correctly. Shop owners use it to set shelf prices from net costs, and to work backwards from a price a customer paid to find the net amount. Accountants use it for quick checks while preparing returns, and anyone buying from abroad uses it to understand how much of a price is tax and how much is the goods themselves. Whenever a price needs VAT added or removed, the tool gives all three figures in one glance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why can I not just subtract 20 percent from a gross price to remove VAT?**

Because the VAT was calculated on the net price, not on the gross price. Subtracting 20 percent of the gross takes off too much. The correct method is to divide the gross price by 1.20, which recovers the net amount, and the tool does that for you.

**Which VAT rate should I use?**

Use the rate that applies in the country and situation you are dealing with. The presets include common rates such as the UK at 20 percent and a European rate at 19 percent, and the custom field accepts any other rate. If you are unsure which rate applies, check with your accountant or tax authority.

**Does the calculator handle prices with cents or pence?**

Yes. The amount field accepts decimals, so you can enter prices with cents or pence and get exact net, tax, and gross figures from the amounts you typed.

**Is anything I type sent anywhere?**

No. Every calculation runs entirely in your browser. The amounts you enter are never uploaded or stored, and closing the tab leaves nothing behind.
