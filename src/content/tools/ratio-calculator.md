## Why Client-Side Matters

Ratios decide whether a photo looks stretched, a recipe scales properly, or a screen fits its frame, and working them out should not involve sending your numbers to a server. This tool solves proportions, scales aspect ratios, and simplifies ratios entirely inside your own browser. Everything you type stays on your device, nothing is uploaded or stored, and the answers appear the moment you type. It is a small piece of maths, and it stays private.

## Step-by-Step Guide

The tool has three tabs, and you start by choosing the one that matches your question. In Proportion Solver, fill in any three of the four numbers and leave the unknown blank; the tool solves for the missing value. In Aspect Ratio Scaler, enter a width and height or pick a preset such as 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, or 9:16, then adjust one dimension and watch the other follow with the ratio locked. In Simplify Ratio, enter two numbers and the tool reduces them to their lowest whole-number form. Every result updates live as you type, so there is no button to press and no waiting.

## Feature Deep Dive

Proportion solving rests on one simple equality: if A is to B as C is to D, then A times D equals B times C. The tool uses cross multiplication to find whichever value you left blank, which is the same method taught in school, applied instantly. Ratio simplification finds the greatest common divisor using the Euclidean algorithm and divides both numbers by it, reducing a ratio to its smallest whole integers. The aspect scaler keeps width and height in fixed proportion, so changing one side recalculates the other instead of stretching the image. Presets cover the ratios people meet most: 16:9 for widescreen video, 4:3 for older screens, 1:1 for square images, and 9:16 for vertical phone video.

Photographers and designers use the aspect scaler to resize images for different screens and social platforms without distortion. Teachers use it for ratio and proportion lessons, and students use it to check homework. Builders and crafters work out scaled dimensions for models and plans, painters mix colours in exact ratios, and anyone printing a photo uses it to find the dimensions that fit a frame without cropping or stretching the image.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I resize an image without stretching it?**

Use the Aspect Ratio Scaler tab. Enter the image's current width and height, or pick the matching preset, then change one dimension. The other dimension adjusts automatically to keep the ratio locked, so the image scales without distortion.

**Can I use decimals in a proportion?**

Yes. Every input box accepts decimal numbers, so you can solve proportions with measurements like 2.5 or 0.75 and get an exact answer from the figures you typed.

**Why does my resized image look stretched?**

Because the width and height were changed independently, breaking the original ratio. Changing only the width, or only the height, distorts the image. Use the aspect scaler to change one side and let the tool calculate the matching other side.

**Is anything I type sent anywhere?**

No. All three tools run entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are never uploaded or stored, and closing the tab leaves nothing behind.
