## Why Client-Side Matters

Your grades and credit hours are your academic record — personal information that should not be sent to a server just to work out an average. This tool runs the entire calculation inside your browser, so the courses and grades you type never leave your device, are never stored, and vanish when you close the tab. You get your GPA and honours standing without an account or any data leaving your machine.

## Step-by-Step Guide

For each course, enter the course title, the letter grade you earned, and the credit hours it carries. Click "+ Add Course" to add as many courses as you need for the semester. If you want your overall standing, you can also enter your prior cumulative GPA and the credits you have already earned, and the tool will fold the new semester into the total. The results appear instantly: your semester GPA, your updated cumulative GPA, and your honours status. Remove or change a course and everything recalculates at once.

## Feature Deep Dive

GPA is total quality points divided by total credit hours. Each letter grade has a point value on the standard 4.0 scale: A is 4.0, A− is 3.7, B+ is 3.3, B is 3.0, B− is 2.7, C+ is 2.3, C is 2.0, D is 1.0, and F is 0.0. Those points are multiplied by the course's credit hours, so a 4-credit course counts double a 2-credit course. The semester GPA covers only the courses you entered; the cumulative GPA blends them with your prior record. Honours status is flagged when your semester GPA reaches 3.50 or higher, the threshold most colleges use for Dean's List.

## Use Cases

Students check their semester GPA the moment grades come out, without waiting for the registrar's system to update. Anyone planning their next term sees how a strong or weak result in one course would move the overall figure. Students close to an honours threshold work out exactly which grades they need to reach it. Transfer students combine a prior GPA with new courses to see where they stand at a new institution. Parents and advisors use the calculator to talk through a student's progress with real numbers instead of guesses.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is a quality point?**

It is the grade's point value multiplied by the course's credit hours. An A in a 3-credit course earns 12 quality points; a B in the same course earns 9. Add up all the quality points and divide by total credits to get the GPA.

**Does a failed course still count in my GPA?**

Yes, on the standard scale. An F earns zero quality points but its credit hours still count in the divisor, so it pulls the average down. That is why a failed course hurts more than simply not taking the course at all.

**Why is my school's GPA slightly different from this one?**

Some schools use a different scale — for example, A+ as 4.3, or plus/minus grades worth different values — or they exclude certain courses. This calculator uses the standard 4.0 scale; if your school differs, the result is a close estimate rather than an exact match.

**Can I use this for high school GPA?**

Yes, if your high school uses the 4.0 scale and credit hours. Some high schools weight honours or advanced courses differently, so check whether your school adds extra points for those before relying on the figure.
