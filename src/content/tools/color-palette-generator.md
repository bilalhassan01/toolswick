## Why Client-Side Matters

A good colour palette makes a design feel finished; a bad one makes even good work look amateur. Finding five colours that sit well together usually means long sessions in a design tool or copying palettes from strangers online. This generator creates harmonious five-colour palettes instantly, in your browser, with nothing uploaded and nothing stored. You can try dozens of combinations in a minute, keep the ones you love, and take the exact hex codes with you.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Building a palette takes seconds. First, press the Spacebar or click Generate New Palette to create a fresh set of five colours. Second, look through the swatches and lock any shade you love by clicking its lock icon — locked colours stay put while the rest change. Third, press Spacebar again to randomise only the unlocked colours, and repeat until the whole palette feels right. Finally, click any colour code to copy it, or use Copy All Hex Codes to grab the full set at once for your stylesheet or design file.

## Feature Deep Dive

The generator builds palettes from colour theory harmonies rather than purely random colours. Complementary pairings sit opposite each other on the colour wheel and create strong contrast, analogous palettes use neighbours on the wheel for a calm, cohesive feel, and monochromatic palettes vary lightness within a single hue for a clean, minimal look. Each palette holds five swatches, a practical size for most designs: a background, a main colour, an accent, a secondary accent, and a text colour. The lock feature is the heart of the tool — find one shade you love, lock it, and let the generator build around it. Every swatch shows its hex code, and clicking it copies the value, with RGB values available too, so the palette drops straight into CSS, Figma, or Photoshop.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I start from a colour I already have?**

The generator creates fresh palettes, but you can lock the closest match and keep regenerating the rest until the set works with your colour. Then check the result against your brand shade with the contrast checker.

**How many palettes can I generate?**

There is no limit. Press Spacebar as many times as you like — each press creates a new set, and locked colours are always kept, so you can explore freely.

**What is the best way to use a five-colour palette?**

A common split is one colour for backgrounds, one main colour, one or two accents for buttons and highlights, and one for text. Keep one accent for the most important action on the page.

**Will these palettes work for a dark theme?**

Yes. Generate palettes until you find a set with a dark shade you like, lock it, and use it as the background. Then check your text colour against it for readability.
