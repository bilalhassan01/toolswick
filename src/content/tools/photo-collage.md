## Why Client-Side Matters

A collage turns a handful of favourite photos into one image worth sharing — a holiday, a birthday, or four angles of the same product. Most collage apps upload your pictures to a server or ask you to install an app full of ads. This tool builds the collage entirely inside your browser, arranging your photos on a canvas in local memory. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on a server, and your private photos stay on your own device.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Building a collage takes a couple of minutes. First, drop two, three, or four photos into the collage workspace, or click to browse and select them. Second, choose your layout: a grid that arranges the photos in a balanced pattern, or stacked rows. Third, adjust the border width slider — from zero for a seamless edge-to-edge look up to 40 pixels — and pick a background frame colour that suits the photos. The tool fits each photo into its cell automatically without stretching it. Finally, click "Download Collage" to save the finished high-resolution image.

## Feature Deep Dive

The collage maker is built around clean, symmetrical layouts for two to four photos. The grid layout gives each photo an equal share of the frame, which suits sets of similar shots, while stacked rows work well when one photo deserves more prominence. Smart aspect-cover fitting fills each cell without distorting the image: instead of squashing a photo to fit, the tool crops the edges slightly so the proportions stay natural. The border control runs from 0 to 40 pixels, so you can choose a tight, modern, edge-to-edge collage or a framed look with a visible gap and a background colour — white for a classic photo-album feel, black for drama, or any colour that matches the pictures. Collages export to a high-resolution 1200 by 1200 canvas, which is sharp enough for social media, messaging apps, and small prints.

## Use Cases

Collages are the quickest way to share a set of photos as one image. Families combine holiday snaps, birthday moments, or a child's firsts into a single picture for a group chat or a framed print. Sellers show four angles of a product in one listing image instead of making buyers scroll. Fitness and renovation journeys work well as collages, with progress photos arranged in order so the change is obvious. Event organisers gather the best moments of a day into one shareable image, and friends make collages of a trip to post on social media, where a single image often gets more attention than a whole album.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does the collage export at 1200 by 1200 pixels?**

That square size is sharp enough for social media, messaging, and small prints, while keeping the file size reasonable. It suits the grid layouts, which are built around a square frame.

**Can I use photos of different shapes in one collage?**

Yes. The aspect-cover fitting fills each cell without stretching, so portrait and landscape photos can sit side by side and still look natural.

**What if I want no border at all between my photos?**

Set the border slider to zero. The photos will sit edge to edge with no gap, giving a clean, seamless collage.

**Is there a limit on how many collages I can make?**

No. You can build as many collages as you like, and each one is processed locally, so there is no queue and no waiting.
