## Why Client-Side Matters

A watermark is how photographers, artists, and sellers mark their work as theirs — but adding one usually means uploading the original photo to a website first. That hands your unprotected, full-quality image to a server you do not control, which is exactly the risk a watermark is meant to guard against. This tool avoids that contradiction entirely. Your photo is loaded into your own browser, the watermark text is drawn onto it on a canvas, and the protected copy downloads straight to your device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on a server, and your original file stays untouched on your disk. The protection is added where your photo already is.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Watermarking a photo takes about a minute. First, select or drop your photo into the watermarking workspace. Second, type your copyright notice or brand name into the text box — your name, your business name, or a short line like a copyright symbol followed by your name and the year. Third, adjust the look: set the font size, pick a colour, move the opacity slider anywhere from 10% to 100%, and choose one of the nine anchor positions on the grid, from a corner to the centre. The preview shows the result as you adjust it, so you can find the balance between visible and unobtrusive. Finally, click Download Watermarked to export the protected photo. Only the exported copy carries the watermark; your original is never altered.

## Feature Deep Dive

The watermark is rendered as a text overlay with a subtle drop shadow, which is what keeps it legible on both dark and light backgrounds — white text with a shadow reads on a bright sky just as well as on a dark room. Opacity is the key control: at 10% the mark is a faint whisper, at 100% it is solid and unmistakable, and the recommended middle ground of 60% to 75% keeps your name clearly visible without hiding the subject underneath. The nine-position anchor grid pins the text to a corner, an edge, or the centre, so you can place it where it interferes least with the composition — or right across the middle when the image needs stronger protection. Font size and colour are adjustable too, so the mark can match your branding. Because everything runs locally in browser memory, there is no upload queue, no waiting, and no copy of your photo left behind on any server.

## Use Cases

Photographers watermark client previews and portfolio shots so their work cannot be reused without credit or payment. Artists protect illustrations and digital paintings before sharing them on social media, where images are routinely reposted without attribution. Online sellers watermark product photos so a competitor cannot lift their catalogue images and present them as their own. Real estate agents mark property photos, and event photographers mark preview galleries, so clients can share and enjoy the pictures while the full-resolution originals stay protected. It also suits anyone posting creative work publicly for the first time — a watermark is a simple, visible statement that the image belongs to someone, and it takes seconds to add before the post goes out.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What should my watermark say?**

Your name, business name, or website is usually enough. Keep it short — a brief line reads clearly at small sizes, while a long sentence becomes hard to read.

**Can I add a logo image instead of text?**

No, this tool adds text watermarks only. You can customise the text with font size, colour, and opacity, but there is no option to upload a logo graphic.

**Will the watermark still show if someone crops the photo?**

A watermark in a corner can be cropped away, so if protection matters more than appearance, place it near the centre or across the subject, where removing it means cutting into the photo itself.

**Can I watermark several photos at once?**

The workspace handles one photo at a time. Watermark each image and download it, then load the next one — the settings stay as you left them.

**Does the watermark stay visible on dark and light photos?**

Yes. The text is drawn with a subtle drop shadow, which keeps it legible on both dark and light backgrounds. You can also adjust the colour and opacity to suit each photo.
