## Why Client-Side Matters

Splitting a bill is a small, everyday piece of maths, and it should not require an account, an app install, or a server. This tool divides a check fairly and instantly, entirely inside your own browser. The amounts you type never leave your device, nothing is uploaded or stored, and nothing remains behind when you close the tab. You can settle up at the table without sharing your spending with anyone.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Start by entering the bill subtotal, before tax and tip. Then add any extra shared charges, and enter the tip and tax as percentages if they apply. Next, enter the number of people sharing the check. The tool immediately shows the exact amount each person owes, including their share of the tip and tax. If you need to share the result, click "Copy Summary" to copy a neat breakdown you can paste straight into a group chat. Change any figure and every amount updates in real time.

## Feature Deep Dive

The calculator splits the bill in a way that is fair to everyone at the table. It starts from the subtotal, adds the tip and tax you entered, and divides the total evenly across the number of people you set. Because tip and tax are entered separately, you can match the real check: a service charge on the bill goes in as a shared charge, while the tip you choose to leave is its own figure. The per-person amount includes everything, so nobody has to do extra maths after the split. The copy summary pulls the whole breakdown together, subtotal, tip, tax, total, and each person's share, so the group can see exactly how the figure was reached and nobody has to take it on trust. It also means the person who paid upfront has a clear record to show everyone, which avoids the awkward follow-up messages the next day.

## Use Cases

Friends use the calculator at restaurants to split a dinner check without arguing over who owes what. Flatmates use it to divide shared takeaway orders, utility bills, and grocery runs. Colleagues use it for team lunches and leaving dos, and travel groups use it to split taxi fares, meals, and shared costs on a trip. It is also useful for parties and group gifts, where one person pays upfront and everyone else pays their share afterwards. Any time a cost is shared, the tool turns a fiddly calculation into a single number everyone can agree on.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What if one person ordered something much more expensive?**

This tool splits the total evenly, which suits groups sharing food or splitting a bill equally. If one person should pay more, work out their extra items separately and split only the shared part with the tool.

**Can I split a bill without adding a tip?**

Yes. Leave the tip percentage at zero and the tool splits just the subtotal and tax. The tip field is optional, so you can use the calculator for any shared cost, tipped or not.

**What happens if the split does not divide evenly?**

The tool shows the exact per-person amount, including cents. In practice, rounding a cent or two up or down between friends is normal, and the copy summary shows the precise figure so everyone can see it.

**Is anything I type sent anywhere?**

No. Every calculation runs entirely in your browser. The amounts you enter are never uploaded or stored, and closing the tab leaves nothing behind.
