## Why Client-Side Matters

Passport and visa photos follow strict rules: exact sizes, a set head height, and the eyes and chin in precise positions. Getting them wrong means a rejected application, a wasted trip to the photo studio, and a delay you did not plan for. Studios and booths charge for every set, and a family applying together pays several times over. This tool lets you crop your own photo to the official dimensions at home, with a biometric guide showing you exactly where your head should sit. And because it runs entirely in your browser, the photo of your face never leaves your device — no upload, no server copy, no account holding your identity photo.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Making a compliant photo takes a few minutes. First, upload a clear, front-facing portrait photo with a plain white background — even, natural lighting and a straight-on angle give the best result. Next, select your destination country's specification from the presets: US 2×2 inch, UK and Schengen 35×45 mm, China 33×48 mm, or Canada 50×70 mm. Then drag and zoom the photo so your eyes and chin line up with the biometric guide oval on the canvas; the guide keeps your head height inside the required range. Finally, choose your output: Single Photo for one image, or the Printable 4×6 inch Sheet, which fits six photos on one sheet. Download your choice and print it at 100% scale with no scaling or fit-to-page adjustment.

## Feature Deep Dive

The tool ships with four country presets, each rendered at 300 DPI for print quality: US at 600×600 pixels, UK and Schengen at 413×531, China at 390×567, and Canada at 590×826. The biometric guide oval is the heart of the tool. Passport authorities typically require the head to occupy 50% to 69% of the image height, with the eyes at a set level — the oval shows you that zone as you drag and zoom, so you can place your face correctly instead of guessing. The canvas supports dragging to pan the photo and a zoom control to scale it, giving fine control over the crop without needing an editor. The 4×6 inch sheet is the money-saver: six photos on a single sheet of standard photo paper, which you can print at a pharmacy or photo kiosk for the price of one print instead of paying per photo at a studio. All of this runs locally through the Canvas API, so your portrait is processed in memory on your own device and never transmitted anywhere.

## Use Cases

The most common use is a passport renewal or a first application, where a correctly sized photo is mandatory and a studio visit is an extra errand. Visa applications are another: different countries want different sizes, and switching presets covers the US, UK, Schengen, China, and Canada without a new photo shoot. Families save the most — one good portrait of each person becomes a full sheet of prints for the price of a single 4×6 print. Frequent travellers keep the tool bookmarked, since a fresh photo is needed every few years and the requirements rarely change. It also helps when an application is urgent: instead of waiting for a studio appointment, you can produce a compliant photo at home in minutes and print it the same day. Students and workers applying for visas, permits, and ID cards use it the same way — one portrait, the right preset, and a printable sheet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What kind of photo should I start with?**

A clear, front-facing portrait with a plain white background, taken in even light with the camera at eye level. The tool handles sizing and cropping, but it cannot fix a sideways angle, a busy background, or a face partly out of frame.

**Does the tool guarantee my photo will be accepted?**

No tool can guarantee acceptance — officials also judge lighting, expression, background, and how recent the photo is. What this tool guarantees is the technical side: exact dimensions, resolution, and head positioning for your preset. Always check your country's official requirements before submitting.

**Can I make passport photos for children?**

Yes, the same presets apply. Take several shots and pick the straightest, most front-facing one, then use the guide oval to position the head correctly.

**What is the biometric guide oval for?**

It shows where your head should sit. Drag and zoom until your eyes and chin line up with the oval, keeping head height inside the 50% to 69% of image height that authorities typically require.

**Can I download just one photo instead of the sheet?**

Yes. Choose Single Photo for one image at the exact preset dimensions, and the 4×6 inch sheet only when you want six copies on one print.
