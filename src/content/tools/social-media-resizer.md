## Why Client-Side Matters

Every social platform crops photos differently, and a picture that looks perfect on your phone can lose someone's head or a key detail once it is posted. Most resizing tools upload your graphic to a server, where a copy can be stored or scanned. This tool resizes your image entirely inside your browser, matching the exact dimensions each platform expects. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on a server, and the finished graphic downloads straight to your device.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Resizing a photo for social media takes a minute. First, upload your graphic or photo into the social resizer. Second, choose your target format from the dropdown — Instagram square or portrait, Instagram Story, Facebook post, YouTube thumbnail, Twitter/X post or header, or LinkedIn post. Third, pick your background fill mode: Blurred Image, which fills the empty space with a soft blur of your own photo; Solid Color, which uses a plain colour; or Crop to Fill, which trims the edges to fit. Finally, click "Download Graphic" to export the image at the exact platform dimensions.

## Feature Deep Dive

The resizer covers the formats people actually need: Instagram square at 1080 by 1080 and portrait at 1080 by 1350, Instagram Story and Reel at 1080 by 1920, Facebook post at 1200 by 630, YouTube thumbnail at 1280 by 720, Twitter/X post at 1200 by 675 and header at 1500 by 500, and LinkedIn post at 1200 by 627. The blurred background fill is the standout feature: it duplicates your photo, blurs it heavily, and places it behind the centred original, so a portrait photo fits a square frame without losing anything — no heads cut off, no text trimmed. Solid colour fill gives a clean, branded frame instead, and Crop to Fill is the right choice when the edges of the photo do not matter and you want the frame completely filled. Every export uses the platform's exact pixel dimensions, so the platform has nothing to crop.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does my photo get cropped when I post it?**

Social platforms crop any image that does not match their aspect ratio. Exporting at the platform's exact dimensions means there is nothing left to crop, so your whole photo stays visible.

**When should I use the blurred background instead of cropping?**

Use the blurred background when the edges of your photo matter — people, text, or products near the frame. It keeps 100 percent of your original visible while filling the required shape.

**Can I make one photo for several platforms at once?**

Yes. Upload the photo once, then export it for each format you need — Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Twitter/X, and LinkedIn — and you will have a correctly sized version for every channel.

**Does resizing change my original photo?**

No. Your original file is never modified. The tool produces a new download at the platform's dimensions, and you can export as many versions as you like from the same upload.
