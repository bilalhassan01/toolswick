## Why Client-Side Matters

A quote card carries your words, your name, and often your photo. Designing one on a server-based tool means uploading your face and your writing to a stranger's system just to get an image back. This tool renders the whole card in your browser, using the text you type and the avatar you choose. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and the finished PNG is a file you own outright, ready to post wherever your audience is.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Start by entering your name, your handle, and the post copy — the quote or announcement you want on the card. Toggle the verified badge if your account is verified. Next, pick a gradient background theme: Indigo, Sunset, Ocean, Dark, or one of the other curated palettes. You can upload your own profile photo, or let the generator create a stylish monogram avatar from your initials. The preview updates live as you type, so you can adjust the wording until it fits. When the card looks right, download it as a high-resolution 1080 by 720 PNG, ready to post.

## Feature Deep Dive

The card exports at 1080 by 720 pixels, a 3:2 ratio that fits feed previews on X and LinkedIn without awkward cropping. The design uses a frosted glass card with soft drop shadows, which gives the text a clean panel to sit on while the gradient shows through around it. Gradient themes are curated rather than random: Electric Indigo for a bold look, Sunset for warmth, Ocean for calm, and Stealth Dark for a serious tone. The avatar system accepts any image you upload, or builds a monogram automatically, so the card looks finished even without a photo. The verified badge toggle adds the familiar checkmark beside your name for accounts that genuinely hold one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will the card look right on Instagram?**

The 3:2 ratio displays as a landscape image in the Instagram feed, which works fine, though the grid preview may crop the edges. The card is designed first for X and LinkedIn, where the full 1080 by 720 frame shows exactly as exported.

**Can I use my logo instead of a profile photo?**

Yes. The avatar upload accepts any square image, so a logo works just as well as a headshot. It will appear in the same circular frame, giving the card a branded rather than personal feel.

**Why is there a verified badge toggle?**

It adds the checkmark beside your name for accounts that are actually verified. Only switch it on if the account holds a real verification — using the badge on an unverified account misleads readers and undermines trust.

**What text length works best?**

Short quotes work best — one or two sentences that fit comfortably on the card. Long paragraphs shrink the text and crowd the design, so if the copy runs long, trim it to the strongest line and let the post caption carry the rest.
