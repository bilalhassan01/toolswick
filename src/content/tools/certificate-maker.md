## Why Client-Side Matters

A certificate marks a real achievement — a finished course, a passed exam, a job well done — and it deserves to look the part. Many certificate sites make you upload names and course details to their servers, or lock the download behind an account. This maker keeps everything on your device. The recipient's name, the course title, and the presenter's details are drawn onto the certificate inside your own browser and never sent anywhere. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and the finished certificate is yours alone.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Creating a certificate takes just a few minutes. First, choose the certificate type: Completion, Achievement, Appreciation, or Excellence. Second, pick an ornamental theme — Imperial Gold, Royal Sapphire, Emerald Prestige, or Classic Formal — and fill in the recipient's full name, the course or award title, the organisation, and the presenter. Third, add the issue date and a short presentation line describing what was accomplished, then check the live preview. Finally, download the certificate as a high-resolution PNG or print it directly from your browser.

## Feature Deep Dive

Certificates are rendered at 1200 by 840 pixels, a high-definition size that stays sharp when printed and framed. The ornamental borders and formal typography are drawn as vectors on the canvas, so lines stay crisp and the layout stays balanced at any size. Each theme is a complete colour scheme: Imperial Gold is warm and traditional, Royal Sapphire is deep and formal, Emerald Prestige is rich and distinctive, and Classic Formal is restrained black and white. The four certificate types change the heading to match the occasion, from a course completed to outstanding work recognised. Because everything is generated locally, you can produce a whole batch of certificates — one per student or employee — without uploading a single name.

## Use Cases

Teachers and trainers issue certificates at the end of a course or workshop, giving students something tangible to keep. Companies recognise employees for training completed, targets hit, or years of service. Webinar hosts send certificates of attendance to participants. Schools and academies award students for academic results, sports, and competitions. Community groups and clubs use them for volunteers and event winners. Because the certificate is a standard image file, it can be printed, framed, emailed, or shared on a professional profile. Online course platforms issue them automatically at scale, and a printed certificate handed over at a graduation ceremony or training day carries far more weight than a plain email saying well done.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I make certificates for a whole class at once?**

The maker creates one certificate at a time, but the process is quick: change the recipient name, download, and repeat. Your other details stay filled in, so each new certificate takes only seconds.

**What should I write in the presentation text?**

Keep it short and specific, such as the course completed or the achievement being recognised. The default text covers course completion, but you can replace it with your own wording for any occasion.

**Does the certificate include a seal or signature line?**

The design uses ornamental borders and formal typography for an official look. You can add a signature by printing the certificate and signing it by hand, which many organisations prefer.

**What paper should I print certificates on?**

Use the Print button with landscape orientation in your print dialog. Heavy paper or card stock gives the best result, and a frame-ready finish suits certificates that will be displayed.
