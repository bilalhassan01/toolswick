## Why Client-Side Matters

Your signature is one of the most personal marks you own. It appears on contracts, forms, and letters, and a copy of it in the wrong hands is a real problem. Drawing it on a website that uploads your strokes to a server would mean a stranger holds a digital copy of your signature. This tool draws entirely into an HTML5 canvas in your own browser. Your strokes never leave your device, nothing is stored, and the transparent PNG you download is yours alone to place where you choose.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Open the tool and draw your signature with a mouse, a touchscreen, or a stylus — smooth curves are drawn with Bézier interpolation, so the line flows like real ink. If you prefer a typed look, switch to Type mode and enter your name to render it in an elegant calligraphy script. Adjust the pen colour — black, navy blue, or red — and the stroke width until the signature looks like yours. When you are happy, download it as a transparent PNG. Because the background is transparent, you can drop the signature onto any document, contract, or form without a white box around it.

## Feature Deep Dive

The drawing engine uses cubic Bézier interpolation, which smooths the raw points from your pointer into flowing curves instead of jagged segments, so the signature looks handwritten rather than drawn by a machine. Touch and pen input are supported natively, with pressure-responsive smoothing on devices that report it, which means a stylus gives a more natural result than a mouse. The Type mode is a genuine alternative rather than an afterthought: it renders your name in calligraphy scripts for a formal, consistent look. Pen colours cover the three classic ink choices — black, navy blue, and red — and stroke width controls the weight of the line. The export is a transparent PNG, so the signature overlays cleanly on any background.

## Use Cases

Freelancers sign PDF contracts and proposals by placing the PNG onto the signature line, instead of printing, signing, and scanning. Small business owners add a signature to invoices and quotes. Anyone filling in a form that accepts an image can sign it digitally in seconds. It also works for email sign-offs, permission slips, and personal letters where a handwritten touch beats a typed name. People who sign documents often keep the downloaded PNG in their files so the same signature is ready whenever it is needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What size should I download the signature at?**

Download it as provided and resize it when you place it in your document. A PNG scales cleanly, so you can shrink it to fit a signature line or enlarge it for a cover letter without redrawing it.

**Can I use this signature legally?**

A drawn image is a representation of your signature, and whether it counts as a valid signature depends on the document type and the laws where you live. For everyday contracts and forms it is widely accepted, but for anything high-stakes, check the local rules first.

**How do I place the signature in a PDF?**

Open the PDF in an editor that supports images, insert the PNG onto the signature line, and resize it to fit. If your editor only accepts Word documents, place the image there and export the document as a PDF when you are done.

**Can I save my signature in the tool for later?**

No, and that is deliberate. Nothing is stored in the tool, so your signature never sits on a server. Download the PNG and keep it in your own files, where only you can reach it.
