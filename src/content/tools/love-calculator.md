## Why Client-Side Matters

A love calculator is pure fun — two names, one percentage, and a verdict to laugh about with friends. But typing your name and your crush's name into a random website is still handing personal details to a server you do not know. This tool calculates your match entirely in your browser. The names are never saved, never sent anywhere, and nothing is stored — the result appears on the page and stays there.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Checking a love match takes seconds. First, enter your name in the first box. Second, enter your partner or crush's name in the second box. Third, click "Calculate Love Match". Finally, read your compatibility score, your relationship rating, and the fun commentary that comes with it — then try another pair of names if you like.

## Feature Deep Dive

The calculator takes the two names you enter and produces a compatibility percentage, a relationship rating, and a short piece of commentary written for fun. The rating ranges from a low match to a perfect "Soulmate" verdict, so every result comes with a label you can share with friends or laugh about together. The score is generated from the names themselves, which means the same pair of names will always give the same result — so if you and a friend both test the same two names, you will get the same answer, which is part of the joke. Because everything runs locally, you can test as many combinations as you like — friends, celebrities, fictional characters — with instant results and no waiting, no account, and no record kept of what you typed.

## Use Cases

Love calculators are a staple of lighthearted fun. Friends test their names together at parties, in group chats, and during school breaks, comparing scores and teasing each other about the results they get. Couples test their own names on anniversaries and Valentine's Day, and single people test a crush's name just for a laugh. They are also a fun addition to social media posts, wedding games, and icebreakers, where a surprising score gets everyone talking and comparing results. The best way to use one is lightly: enjoy the result, share it if it makes you smile, and do not take the number seriously — it is a game, not a verdict on a real relationship, and the real test of any relationship is how the two people treat each other every day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do the same two names always give the same score?**

Because the score is generated from the names themselves, not from a random draw. That way the result is consistent — test the same pair twice and you will always get the same answer back.

**Can I test names of celebrities or fictional characters?**

Yes. You can enter any two names you like, so celebrity couples, book characters, and film pairings all work — and the results are often much funnier than real ones.

**Is my crush's name stored anywhere?**

No. The names are processed in your browser and never sent to a server or saved anywhere at all. Close the tab and nothing remains at all.

**What does a low score mean?**

Nothing at all. The calculator is a novelty tool for entertainment, and a low score is just part of the fun — many great real-life couples would score terribly on it.
