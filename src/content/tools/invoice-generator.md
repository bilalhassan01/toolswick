## Why Client-Side Matters

An invoice is a business document that carries your rates, your client's details, and your money — exactly the information you least want sitting on a stranger's server. Many invoice tools store your billing history online, or lock PDF export behind an account. This generator builds the whole invoice inside your browser. Your business details, client addresses, and rates stay on your device, and the finished invoice is yours to print or save as a PDF, with nothing uploaded and nothing stored.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Creating an invoice takes a few minutes. First, fill in your business details and your client's contact information, and add your logo if you have one. Second, add billable items one by one — description, quantity, and unit rate — and watch the subtotal update automatically. Third, set your currency, then add a tax rate, a discount, or a shipping charge if they apply; the totals recalculate in real time. Finally, click Print / Save as PDF and choose Save as PDF in your browser's print destination menu.

## Feature Deep Dive

Every number on the invoice is calculated live, so there is no mental arithmetic and no spreadsheet to maintain. Item rows multiply quantity by rate automatically, and the subtotal feeds into tax, discount, and shipping to produce the grand total. Seven currencies are supported — USD, EUR, GBP, JPY/CNY, INR, CAD, and AUD — with the correct symbol applied throughout the document. Tax is set as a percentage, discounts can be applied to the total, and a shipping line covers delivery charges. You can add as many item rows as the job needs and remove any row that does not belong. The layout is clean and professional, designed to print well, and the PDF export uses your browser's own print-to-PDF, so the file you send a client looks exactly like the preview.

## Use Cases

Freelancers bill clients for projects, retainers, and hourly work without paying for accounting software. Small businesses invoice customers for products, repairs, and services. Consultants send itemised bills that show exactly what each line covers. Tutors, designers, and tradespeople produce a professional invoice on the spot, straight after finishing a job. Anyone who needs a one-off invoice — a single sale, a refund note, a deposit request — can make one in minutes instead of building a template from scratch. Landlords invoice tenants for repairs or shared costs, and clubs and societies bill members for events, trips, and annual subscriptions with the same tool.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I save an invoice and come back to it later?**

The generator works in your browser session, so it is best to print or save each invoice as a PDF when you finish it. Keep the PDF as your record, and start a fresh invoice for the next client.

**How do I number my invoices?**

Add an invoice number in the details section and keep your own sequence — for example, starting at 001 and counting up. A simple running list in a spreadsheet is enough to stay organised.

**Can I add my logo to the invoice?**

Yes. Upload your logo image and it will appear on the invoice, giving it a professional, branded look that clients recognise at a glance.

**What if my client needs the invoice in a different currency?**

Choose the currency from the dropdown before printing. The symbol updates throughout the invoice, so the document is consistent from the first line to the grand total.
