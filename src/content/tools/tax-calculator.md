## Why Client-Side Matters

Prices rarely tell the whole story. A shelf price, an invoice total, or a supplier's quote may already include tax, or it may not, and the difference matters when you are budgeting, invoicing a client, or checking a receipt. Most online tax tools ask you to type your amounts into a form on someone else's server, which means your financial figures travel across the internet and sit in logs you cannot see. This calculator works differently. Every figure you enter is processed entirely inside your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on a server, and nothing remains behind after you close the tab.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Using the calculator takes less than a minute, and every step happens on the same page. First, choose your mode: "Add Tax" if you have a net price and want the gross total, or "Remove Tax" if you have a gross price and want the amount before tax. Second, enter the amount in the amount box, exactly as it appears on the invoice or price tag. Third, pick your currency symbol from the selector, which offers dollars, euros, pounds, yen, and rupees. Fourth, select or enter your tax percentage rate. The result appears as an itemised breakdown showing the net amount, the tax amount, and the gross total, so you can see each figure separately. Finally, copy the breakdown with one click for an invoice, a message, or your records.

## Feature Deep Dive

The calculator handles both directions of the same problem, and the maths behind each direction is different in a way that catches people out. Adding tax is straightforward: the gross price equals the net price multiplied by one plus the rate divided by one hundred, so a net price of 100 at a rate of 20 becomes a gross price of 120. Removing tax is not simply a matter of subtracting the same percentage from the gross price, because the rate was originally applied to the smaller net figure. The tool divides the gross price by one plus the rate divided by one hundred, which recovers the exact net amount, and then subtracts that net figure from the gross to find the tax. That way, applying the rate to the calculated net returns the original gross exactly. The rate field accepts any percentage you enter, so the tool works for sales tax, VAT, and GST alike, and everything runs locally, so the result appears instantly.

## Use Cases

This calculator earns its keep in everyday money tasks. Freelancers use it when preparing invoices, adding tax to a net fee so the client sees a correct gross total, or working backwards from a gross payment to find out how much of it was actually their fee. Shopkeepers and small sellers use it to check shelf prices, making sure the price a customer pays includes the right amount of tax without guessing. Anyone who has received a quote or a receipt can use it to check whether the tax figure on the document is right, by entering the gross total and the rate and comparing the breakdown with what is printed. It is also handy when comparing prices across sellers, since one price may be quoted net and another gross, and only a like-for-like comparison shows which is genuinely cheaper. People preparing expense claims use it to separate the tax from a gross receipt, so the claim shows the net cost and the tax as distinct lines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What tax rate should I enter?**

Enter the rate that applies to the price you are checking. Rates differ by place, by product, and by the type of tax, so check the rate on the receipt or invoice rather than guessing.

**Why is the tax I remove smaller than the rate times the gross price?**

Because the rate was originally applied to the net price, not the gross price. Removing tax means dividing the gross price by one plus the rate, which recovers the net figure first, and the tax is the difference between the two.

**Can I calculate tax for several items at once?**

Yes. Add the item prices together first, then enter the combined total as the amount. The calculator works on whatever single figure you give it, so a summed total produces the correct tax for the whole set of items.

**Does the tool save or send my amounts?**

No. Every calculation happens inside your browser, and your figures are never uploaded or stored anywhere. Closing the tab leaves nothing behind.

**Does it work without an internet connection once the page has loaded?**

Yes. The calculation runs entirely on your device, so once the page is open you can keep using it even if your connection drops.
