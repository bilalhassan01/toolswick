## Why Client-Side Matters

Splitting a restaurant bill should be the easiest part of the evening, yet it is the moment when phones come out, mental arithmetic fails, and someone ends up paying too much. Most online tip tools are wrapped in pages that track what you type and set cookies, all for a sum that takes seconds to work out. This calculator keeps it simple. Your bill amount, your tip percentage, and the size of your party are processed entirely inside your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on a server, and there are no cookies or tracking attached to the calculation.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Working out a tip here takes a few seconds, and every step happens on the same page. First, enter the bill subtotal before tip, exactly as it appears on the receipt. Second, choose a tip percentage. The quick presets cover 15, 18, and 20 percent, or you can type any custom rate you prefer. Third, set the party size if you are splitting the bill among several guests; the per-person figure updates to match. Fourth, read the result, which shows the total tip, the grand total including tip, and the amount each person owes. If you want a tidier number, use the round-up option to bring the total to the nearest whole dollar. Finally, copy the breakdown with one click if you want to share it with the table or keep it for your records.

## Feature Deep Dive

The calculator is built around three figures and one clear rule: the tip is worked out from the bill subtotal you enter, and everything else follows from that. Entering a subtotal before tip means the percentage applies to the food and drink alone, which is how most people prefer to tip, and the result shows the tip amount and the grand total as separate lines so you can check both. The preset buttons for 15, 18, and 20 percent cover the common choices in one tap, while the custom rate field accepts any percentage, so you are never forced into a preset that does not suit the service. Splitting is handled by dividing the grand total evenly among the number of guests you enter, and the per-person figure is shown alongside the total so nobody has to do the division themselves. A currency selector offers dollars, euros, pounds, yen, and rupees, so the figures read in the money you are actually spending. The round-up option adjusts the total to the nearest whole dollar, and because the calculation runs locally, every change updates the result instantly.

## Use Cases

The tip calculator is most at home at the restaurant table, but it turns up in plenty of other places too. A group of friends sharing a meal can settle the bill in seconds, with each person seeing exactly what they owe instead of arguing over rough shares. A traveller in a country where tipping customs differ can check what a given percentage adds to the bill before deciding what feels right. Someone on a budget can test different percentages and see immediately how each one changes the total, which makes it easier to enjoy the meal without worrying about the bill afterwards. It is also useful for delivery orders and takeaway, where the total on the screen is not always the total you will pay once a tip is added. Small business owners who entertain clients can work out the full cost of a meal, tip included, before the evening starts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I split the bill unevenly?**

The tool splits the grand total evenly among the number of guests you enter, so each person pays the same share. If people ordered very different amounts, work out each person's share separately by entering their own subtotal.

**Does the calculator include tax in the tip?**

The tip is calculated on the subtotal you enter. If you type the bill before tax, the tip is worked out on that figure. If you type a total that already includes tax, the tip will be calculated on the larger figure instead.

**What if the bill already includes a service charge?**

Check the receipt first. If a service charge is already included, that is the tip in most cases, and adding another percentage on top would double it. You can set the rate to zero to see the total without any extra tip.

**Can I use a currency other than dollars?**

Yes. The currency selector offers dollars, euros, pounds, yen, and rupees, and the result is shown with the symbol you choose. The maths is the same whichever currency you pick.

**Does it remember my last bill?**

No. The calculator does not store anything, and there are no cookies or tracking attached to it. Each visit starts fresh, and your figures disappear when you close the tab.
