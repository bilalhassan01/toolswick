## Why Client-Side Matters

Converting a duration is a small piece of maths that should be instant and private. This tool converts time units entirely inside your own browser, with no server involved and no data leaving your device. The numbers you type are never uploaded or stored, and the result appears the moment you type it. There is no account, no waiting, and nothing left behind when you close the tab.

## Step-by-Step Guide

Enter the duration into the input field, then select the unit you are converting from, such as hours, minutes, seconds, or milliseconds. Next, choose the unit you want to convert to, and the converted duration appears immediately. A comparison table below the result shows your value in every supported unit at once, so you can check several conversions without starting over. If you need the result elsewhere, copy it from the output box, and change any input at any time to see the answer update in real time.

## Feature Deep Dive

The converter covers durations from milliseconds up to calendar years. The second is the base unit of time in the international system, defined by the vibrations of cesium-133 atoms, and every other unit is built from it. Days, weeks, and hours follow exact definitions: a day is 86,400 seconds and a week is 168 hours. Calendar units are trickier, because real years and months vary in length, so the tool uses standard averages: a year is treated as 365.25 days, or 31,557,600 seconds, which accounts for leap years, and a month is treated as 30.4375 days, the average length of a calendar month. Those averages make year and month conversions consistent, though for exact dates a date calculator is the better tool. Weeks are exact, since a week is always seven days, which is why converting weeks to hours or days never involves rounding or approximation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does converting months to days give a decimal?**

Because real months have different lengths, from 28 to 31 days. The tool uses the average month of 30.4375 days, so a conversion like three months gives a decimal result rather than a whole number of days. For exact calendar dates, use a date calculator instead.

**Can I convert years into seconds?**

Yes. Enter the number of years and choose seconds as the target unit. The tool uses the standard astronomical year of 365.25 days, so one year converts to 31,557,600 seconds, accounting for leap years.

**What is the smallest unit the converter supports?**

Milliseconds, which are thousandths of a second. One hour contains 3,600,000 milliseconds, and the converter handles them exactly, which is useful for timing, computing, and scientific work.

**Is anything I type sent anywhere?**

No. Every conversion runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are never uploaded or stored, and closing the tab leaves nothing behind.
