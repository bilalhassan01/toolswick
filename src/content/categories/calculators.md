# Calculators

From working out a discount while shopping to planning a mortgage, most everyday decisions come down to a quick calculation. This collection of 46 calculators and converters covers money, health, dates, and units in one place, so students, shoppers, travellers, homeowners, and small business owners can get a clear answer without reaching for a spreadsheet. Every result appears instantly as you type, with the working shown where it helps you check the maths.

The calculators use recognised standard formulas rather than rough estimates. Calorie needs are calculated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, body fat uses the official US Navy circumference method, and BMI results use the World Health Organization weight categories. Grades follow the standard 4.0 GPA scale, Zakat is calculated at the standard 2.5% rate, and unit converters use exact conversion factors, including the binary 1024 standard for data storage. Converters cover length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, data, time, pressure, energy, angle, power, and frequency, so one page handles nearly any unit you meet. All of the maths runs in your browser, so your salary, weight, or loan figures are never sent anywhere.

Start with these popular tools:

- **Percentage Calculator** — work out percentages, increases, and decreases in seconds.
- **BMI Calculator** — check your Body Mass Index in metric or imperial units.
- **Loan Calculator** — see monthly payments, total interest, and the full repayment schedule.
- **Age Calculator** — get your exact age in years, months, and days, plus days lived.
- **Mortgage Calculator** — plan monthly payments including taxes and insurance.
