# Audio Tools

Musicians, podcasters, teachers, and language learners all need simple audio jobs done quickly: hearing a written passage read aloud, keeping a steady beat while practising, testing a speaker, or trimming a clip into a ringtone. This collection of 4 audio tools does exactly that, entirely in your browser. There is no software to install, no audio to upload to a server, and no account standing between you and the sound you need.

Every tool here is powered by your browser's own audio engine. The Metronome keeps time with the high-precision clock of the Web Audio API, the Tone Generator synthesises pure sine, square, sawtooth, and triangle waves with the same API's built-in oscillator, and the Audio Cutter decodes and trims files by slicing Web Audio API audio buffers, so your music and voice notes never leave your device. Text to Speech uses the browser's native SpeechSynthesis engine, reading your text aloud with the voices already installed on your device and letting you adjust speed and pitch as you listen.

Here are the four tools in this collection:

- **Text to Speech** — turn written text into spoken audio for proofreading or language practice.
- **Online Metronome** — practise with a precise click from 30 to 260 beats per minute.
- **Tone Generator** — play pure test tones from 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz for speakers and hearing checks.
- **Audio Cutter** — trim songs and voice clips into ringtones, intros, and short snippets.

Pick the tool you need below and press play — each one works on desktop and mobile, so you can practise, test, or trim wherever you are.
