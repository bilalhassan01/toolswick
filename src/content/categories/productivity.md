# Productivity

Some decisions are best left to chance, and some skills are worth measuring, and this collection of 8 productivity tools covers both. Teachers can pick a raffle winner fairly, game nights can roll dice when the physical ones are lost, and anyone can test their typing speed, reaction time, or clicking speed in under a minute. They are handy for classrooms, game nights, live streams, and quick team decisions alike. Everything runs instantly in the browser, so there is nothing to download, install, or sign up for before you start.

Fairness is the point of the random tools, so they use the browser's cryptographic entropy rather than predictable pseudo-random numbers, giving every name in a list and every side of a coin a genuinely equal chance. The skill tests run entirely on your device as well: the Typing Speed Test measures words per minute and accuracy in real time, the Reaction Time Test records your reflexes in milliseconds, and the Click Speed Test counts your clicks per second over timed rounds. No results are uploaded or stored anywhere, so a classroom draw stays honest and a practice score stays yours alone.

Start with these popular tools:

- **Random Picker** — draw fair winners and random items from any list you paste in.
- **Typing Speed Test** — measure your typing speed and accuracy with 60-second tests.
- **Dice Roller** — roll D4 through D100 dice for board games and tabletop role-playing.
- **Reaction Time Test** — check your visual reflex speed, measured in milliseconds.
